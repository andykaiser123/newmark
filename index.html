Analyst notes

Projection assumptions: market rents begin only after BOTH renovation completion and stabilization. Prior to that, current rents are used. Value is based on annualized NOI and a constant cap rate; actual value may differ and renovation does not guarantee a value jump. Rent and expense growth are modeled independently. Cash flow excludes taxes, sale costs, and refinancing. All calculations are illustrative. No external market verification or lender sizing is performed. Financing is based on purchase price, renovation paid in cash, and expenses are a percentage of collected rent. Saved scenarios are kept in this browser only.
